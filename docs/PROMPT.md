## Overview

The RPG should run on the web and be a PWA so it can be installed and played on mobile devices.  
It will be played primarily on mobile devices. It is a mystery RPG that uses AI NPCs
to drive the story. Users will have a character that they operate, navigating the character
throughout the map interacting with game objects to talk with NPCs and solve puzzles, riddles,
and eventually solve the mystery.

## Puzzles

- Classic detective work. Find clues (game items), talk with NPCs
  Example: You find a note in the grocery store written from one worker to another saying "You're not seriously considering joining Mr.Arthur's club are you?"
  Mr. Arthur is the art museum owner. Talk to grocery store employees about Mr. Arthur and his 'club'. The worker's reluctantly let slip that if you say a secret
  passphrase (some cryptic question) to Mr. Arthur, he will take you to the basement of the art museum and make you a member of his secret society. Go to Mr. Arthur, say the passphrase, he brings you to the basement which unlocks that part of the map.
- Open safe.
  Example: You find graffiti of an ATM where the pin pad shows 4 numbers worn out. You find a safe that takes a 4 digit pin. Entering the correct order of the 4 digits from the graffiti will unlock the safe (and lead to some other clue unraveling the mystery).
  !TASK: write a puzzles.md outlining each puzzle, any needed game mechanics for them and how they enrich and keep the story moving forward.

## Mystery Story

Story should be constructed using character centric GMC (Goal Motivation Conflict).
Main character should quickly be motivated to complete some 'goal' (G).
This is the heart of the mystery. Could be solving a crime or
unraveling strange happenings or behaviors in the town.
The character should have some instilled 'motivation' (M) for completing the goal.
There should be some natural and character driven conflict (C) that makes the
character struggle to complete the goal.
