## Proposed development prototype description

Play Detective Riley in an early mystery RPG prototype. Explore a town and library,
meet NPCs and try local-AI conversations. Movement, inventory and checkpoints are
implemented. The mystery campaign and ending are still under development.

Local AI requires a separately configured supported backend. Without it, NPCs use
limited scripted greetings and replies that do not award clues. Browser-only
players should expect an exploration prototype, not a finishable offline mystery.

Verified from source: arrow/WASD movement and click-to-move; Space opens quick
speech, Enter sends and Escape closes it. Native interaction remains to be tested.
No cross-browser, 60 FPS, combat or branching-story claim is made.

Suggested tester distribution: free, development status, no promised paid content.
Before uploading: verify asset licenses, exact build/hash, normal browser and
itch iframe gameplay, source-to-package consistency and actual supported inputs.
Use docs/GAMEPLAY-AUDIT.md and the human tester packet as acceptance criteria.
