# Characters

## Player Character

**Detective Grace (Female)** - Former city detective who moved to this small town for a quieter life. Drawn into mystery when strange events begin occurring.

## Police Station

**Sheriff Martinez** - 20-year veteran, knows everyone in town. Protective of town's reputation but ultimately wants truth.
**Deputy Charlie Sirrine** - Young, eager, tech-savvy. More willing to share information than Sheriff.

## Hospital

**Dr. Sarah Chen** - Head physician, notices patterns in recent unusual cases. Bound by patient confidentiality but drops hints.
**Nurse Williams** - Night shift nurse who's seen strange things. Nervous but helpful if approached correctly.
**Receptionist Janet** - Town gossip hub, knows everyone's business. Guards medical records but can be convinced to help.

## School

**Principal Thompson** - Concerned about student safety, has noticed behavioral changes in some students.
**Ms. Rodriguez** - History teacher researching local legends. Has knowledge of town's past that connects to current mystery.
**Mr. Kim** - Science teacher who's noticed missing lab supplies. Suspicious of after-school activities.
**Alex** - Student leader, popular, seems to know more than they're saying.
**Maya** - Quiet student who observes everything, has seen suspicious meetings.
**Jordan** - Tech-savvy student who's hacked into systems and found disturbing information.

## Grocery Store

**Tom (Day Manager)** - Straightforward, notices unusual purchasing patterns.
**Lisa (Night Clerk)** - Sees who comes and goes after hours, knows about secret meetings.
**Marcus (Stock Boy)** - Young, impressionable, was approached to join "the club" but declined.

## Art Museum

**Emma (Tour Guide)** - Enthusiastic about local history, unknowingly provides clues through historical knowledge.
**Mr. Arthur (Owner)** - Mysterious recluse who leads secret society. Appears harmless but harbors dark secrets.
