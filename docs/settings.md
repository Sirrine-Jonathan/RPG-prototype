# Settings

## Police Station
**NPCs:** Sheriff Martinez, Deputy Collins
**Significance:** Central hub for official investigation. Contains evidence locker, case files, and holding cells. Key location for accessing official reports and getting law enforcement perspective on the mystery.

## Hospital
**NPCs:** Dr. Sarah Chen, Nurse Williams, Receptionist Janet
**Significance:** Medical records and witness testimonies from recent incidents. Contains clues about unusual symptoms or injuries that tie to the mystery. Restricted areas require puzzle-solving to access.

## School (K-12)
**NPCs:** Principal Thompson, Ms. Rodriguez (History Teacher), Mr. Kim (Science Teacher), Students: Alex, Maya, Jordan
**Significance:** Information network through students and staff. School records, after-school activities, and student gossip provide crucial leads. Science lab contains materials relevant to mystery.

## Grocery Store
**NPCs:** Day Manager Tom, Night Clerk Lisa, Stock Boy Marcus
**Significance:** Community gathering place where secrets are shared. Employee break room contains the note about Mr. Arthur's club. Security cameras may hold evidence.

## Local Art Museum
**NPCs:** Tour Guide Emma, Museum Owner Mr. Arthur (reclusive, lives upstairs)
**Significance:** Houses the secret society meeting place in basement. Contains artifacts and paintings that hold clues. Mr. Arthur's apartment above museum is key location.

## Stone Cave (Outside Town)
**NPCs:** None (initially)
**Significance:** Final location where mystery culminates. Contains ancient symbols, hidden chambers, and the truth behind the town's strange occurrences. Unlocked late in game.
