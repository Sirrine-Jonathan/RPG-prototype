# Whispering Stones

## GMC Structure

### Goal (G)

Detective Riley must uncover the truth behind a series of strange disappearances and behavioral changes in the small town, ultimately stopping an ancient ritual that threatens to unleash something dangerous from the stone cave.

### Motivation (M)

Riley moved to this quiet town to escape the trauma of a failed case in the city where they couldn't save someone. When people start disappearing and acting strangely, Riley is compelled to act - both to protect the innocent townspeople and to prove to themselves they can solve a case that matters.

### Conflict (C)

- **External:** The secret society actively works to hide their activities and mislead the investigation
- **Internal:** Riley struggles with self-doubt from past failures and must overcome fear of failing again
- **Social:** Town residents are reluctant to talk, either from fear or loyalty to society members
- **Time Pressure:** The ritual has a deadline tied to astronomical events

## Detailed Story Progression with Puzzle Integration

### Chapter 1: The First Disappearance

**Starting Locations:** Police Station, Hospital, School, Grocery Store, Museum (lobby only)

Riley arrives to investigate Maya's disappearance. Sheriff Martinez is dismissive, Deputy Collins more helpful but constrained.

**Puzzle 1 - Medical Records Access:**

- Visit hospital, befriend Janet the receptionist
- **Unlock:** Access to patient database reveals pattern of memory loss cases
- **Story Impact:** Establishes supernatural element, unlocks deeper hospital conversations
- **NPC Changes:** Dr. Chen now discusses "unusual neurological symptoms" instead of being evasive

### Chapter 2: The Secret Network

**New Dialogue Options Unlocked:** NPCs now reference "the club" when trust is built

**Puzzle 2 - The Secret Society Passphrase:**

- Find note in grocery store break room about "Mr. Arthur's club"
- Talk to Marcus (stock boy) who reveals he was approached but declined
- Lisa (night clerk) hints at the passphrase: "Ask him about the stones that whisper"
- **Unlock:** Museum basement access
- **Story Impact:** Reveals secret society meeting place
- **NPC Changes:** Mr. Arthur transforms from eccentric recluse to cryptic cult leader

### Chapter 3: Deeper Investigation

**New Location Unlocked:** Museum basement with ritual chamber

**Puzzle 3 - The Worn ATM Code:**

- Basement contains safe with ritual instructions
- ATM graffiti downtown shows worn numbers: 3, 7, 1, 9
- **Unlock:** Safe reveals membership list and ritual timeline
- **Story Impact:** Identifies society members, reveals 3-day countdown to ritual
- **NPC Changes:** Known members (Tom, Principal Thompson) become evasive when confronted

**Puzzle 4 - School Lab Break-in:**

- Membership list includes Mr. Kim (science teacher)
- Sneak into school after hours, find missing chemicals inventory
- **Unlock:** Understanding of ritual components (memory-altering substances)
- **Story Impact:** Explains the "dazed" townspeople behavior
- **NPC Changes:** Students Alex and Jordan now share they've seen teachers acting strange

### Chapter 4: The Conspiracy Deepens

**New Dialogue Trees:** NPCs split into "believers" and "resisters"

**Puzzle 5 - Sheriff's Evidence Locker:**

- Deputy Collins reveals Sheriff has been hiding evidence
- Create distraction, access locker during shift change
- **Unlock:** Confiscated items include stone fragments with symbols
- **Story Impact:** Sheriff Martinez revealed as society member, Deputy becomes ally
- **NPC Changes:** Deputy Collins now actively helps investigation

**Puzzle 6 - Hospital Security Footage:**

- Night nurse Williams shows footage of late-night "treatments"
- **Unlock:** Visual proof of society members bringing victims to hospital
- **Story Impact:** Dr. Chen revealed as unwilling participant under coercion
- **NPC Changes:** Dr. Chen becomes informant, reveals cave location and ritual details

### Chapter 5: The Ancient Truth

**New Location Unlocked:** Stone Cave (exterior and first chamber)

**Puzzle 7 - Cave Symbol Translation:**

- Collect symbol rubbings from basement, evidence locker, and cave entrance
- Ms. Rodriguez (history teacher) helps translate ancient warnings
- **Unlock:** Inner cave chambers and understanding of the entity
- **Story Impact:** Reveals the "Whispering Stones" are a prison for an ancient consciousness
- **NPC Changes:** Ms. Rodriguez joins as research ally, provides historical context

### Chapter 6: Race Against Time

**All Locations Accessible:** Full cave system, all building interiors

The ritual is tonight. Society members become hostile if confronted. Riley must:

- Rally allies (Deputy Collins, Dr. Chen, Ms. Rodriguez, students Maya and Jordan)
- Gather evidence to expose the conspiracy
- Reach the cave before the ritual completes

**Final Confrontation:**

- Cave's inner sanctum during the ritual
- Mr. Arthur attempting to merge with the ancient entity
- Missing townspeople as intended vessels
- Riley must use knowledge from all puzzles to disrupt the ritual and seal the entity

## Progression Mechanics

### Location Unlocks

1. **Hospital Database** → Medical Records Puzzle
2. **Museum Basement** → Secret Society Puzzle
3. **Cave Exterior** → Symbol Translation Puzzle
4. **Cave Interior** → All previous puzzles completed

### NPC Dialogue Evolution

- **Suspicious** → **Helpful** (after building trust)
- **Evasive** → **Informative** (after presenting evidence)
- **Neutral** → **Allied** or **Hostile** (based on society membership)

### Story Gates

Each puzzle completion unlocks new story information and changes how NPCs respond, creating a branching narrative that converges on the final revelation.
