🧠 Smart NPC AI System

Autonomous NPCs with line of sight, discovery, and AI-driven behavior

AI-Driven NPC Features:

  • IDLE State: NPCs autonomously explore, discover objects, and make decisions
  • Line of Sight: 2D raycasting with obstacle detection
  • Discovery System: NPCs remember objects and characters they've seen
  • Dynamic Tools: Available actions change based on discovered objects
  • AI Decision Making: LLM chooses actions based on personality and context
  • ENGAGED State: NPCs can be engaged in conversation (30s timeout)

NPC Actions:

  • 🔍 Look: North/East/South/West to explore
  • 🚶 Move: To different locations in the room
  • 💬 Speak: Initiate conversations with discovered characters
  • 🔧 Interact: Use tools on discovered objects (examine, hide evidence, etc.)
  • ⏸️ Wait: Observe surroundings

Watch the console to see AI decision-making in real-time!
